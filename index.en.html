Survival Action RPG

Bit-Rate-Rush

One knife. An endless tide. Every minute you survive, the town learns your name a little better.

A 2D pixel-art RPG that layers a town, quests and a story on top of "move-to-fight" survival action. Survive a few minutes, go home, earn, gear up, take the next job — and get closer to what is really happening in this world.

Overview

Keep moving. The weapons fire themselves.

On the battlefield you do one thing: keep moving. Weapons throw themselves, magic burns on its own. All you choose is where to run and what to grow next. A job takes 3–10 minutes — built for handhelds, one job at a time.

Story

When the rate rises, the Rush comes.

Every living thing in this world carries bits — tiny motes of light. Slay a monster and its bits scatter on the wind. No human can gather them.

The world also has a rate — the speed of its flow — and when someone raises it, sleeping monsters wake without end and pour toward the town. People call it the Rush, and have feared the word so long they no longer say it aloud.

Now bats are darkening the sky over the castle town of Lumen.

— Since the day a young man walked through its gate.

His village beyond the mountain was swallowed by a Rush. He reached the town alone, with nothing but a knife. The tavern master calls him "new face"; the innkeeper tells him to rest — but he has no coin. So he stands before the job board.

Play

Gear up in town, run in the field, come home.

Tavern

Pick a job from the board: survive, or hunt. The master's rumours point to the next chapter.

Weaponsmith & store

Buy weapons with the coins you found and raise their rank. Carry up to three consumables.

Inn

Wounds follow you home. Healing costs coin. Keeping the journal saves the game.

Castle

The King's special requests, rewarded with royal relics — magic. Weapon slots grow with the chapters.

The field

Move only. Gather bits, and pick one of three upgrades on every level up.

Go home, or push your luck

Clear the job and leave — or keep fighting for coin. A knockout halves what you bring back.

World

Five chapters, five battlefields.

CHAPTER 1

Departure

CHAPTER 2
